# Crit 10 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough came from asking how more people could be in the garden at once without it feeling crowded. The obvious fix was to make the single bed much bigger, but I decided to add three more beds instead and give each one a theme: a cottage garden, a flower garden, a fruit garden and a vegetable garden. People can now spread out instead of competing for plots in one bed. The server enforces each bed's theme, so a plant that doesn't fit gets turned away no matter what the page shows. That rule is written down in the repo with the reasons for it.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This work made me think about how I want to design for how people actually behave. Instead of adding pressure, I gave them more places to go, so they spread out on their own. I want to make things that are fun and satisfying to use, and keep a relaxing game from turning into something stressful.
