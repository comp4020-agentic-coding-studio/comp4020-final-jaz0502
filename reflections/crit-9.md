# Crit 9 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough came from asking how visible other people were in the garden. Before, you could watch plants change, but you couldn't tell whether anyone else was there at all. So I brought back what I had left out of the first version: figures. Each gardener now has a figure that walks the path around the beds, and a list shows who is here, so you can see how many people are online. Gardeners can also wave to each other, which gives you something to respond to as well as something to watch. The garden is still cooperative, with no scoring. I added a private profile panel that shows each gardener a plain record of what they have planted, watered and composted, and nobody else can see it.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This work made me think more about how an app can be interactive between people while still maintaining privacy. Other gardeners only ever appear through a one-way public id, never someone's real identifier. I want to build multiplayer experiences that are also collaborative rather than competitive, where people share a space instead of playing against each other.
