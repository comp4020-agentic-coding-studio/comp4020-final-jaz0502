# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was working out how to build something with no scoring system, just a collaborative shared space that people can use together. I came up with a few ideas, including a shared art gallery. When I thought about how to make the gallery collaborative, I considered letting people edit each other's work, almost like one large painting. But I realised this could lead to chaos, with people destroying each other's work. That pushed me towards a shared garden instead. In the garden, other people can help but not destroy: anyone can water any plot, but nobody can overwrite or remove someone else's healthy plant. That is the idea the whole app is built around.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This work made me think more about how I want to make games and apps that are fun and easy to use, without making the user read through a large set of instructions. I want to create things that are genuinely enjoyable by people of all ages.
